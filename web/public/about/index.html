REDDIT, 2015 → SOLANA, 2026

A button became a society.

Reddit's 2015 April Fools experiment gave eligible accounts one irreversible press and a shared 60-second countdown. Every press reset the clock. The second you chose became your color. The timer ultimately reached zero and the experiment ended.

BUTTON keeps only the primitive: one username, one press, one shared clock, one irreversible ending.
WHY NO WALLET

A username is the whole price of entry.

No wallet, no gas, no signature. You pick a username, and the shared clock and every press live on the site's own server. That means you trust the site to keep the clock honest, the same as any other website.

WHY NO REWARDS

Because incentives ruin the question.

BUTTON does not pay you to press, gate the button behind token ownership, or promise an airdrop. The token launching on Solana changes nothing about who can press or what faction they land in. The interesting behavior is what people do when the only scarce thing is their one irreversible action.
