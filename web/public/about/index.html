REDDIT, 2015 → ROBINHOOD CHAIN, 2026

A button became a society.

Reddit's 2015 April Fools experiment gave eligible accounts one irreversible press and a shared 60-second countdown. Every press reset the clock. The second you chose became your color. The timer ultimately reached zero and the experiment ended.

BUTTON keeps only the primitive: one wallet, one press, one shared clock, one irreversible ending.
WHY ONCHAIN

The rules do not need us.

The countdown state, one-press constraint and press history live in a public contract. The website is only an interface. If it disappears, the experiment is still readable and callable.

WHY NO REWARDS

Because incentives ruin the question.

BUTTON does not pay you to press, gate the button behind token ownership, or promise an airdrop. The interesting behavior is what people do when the only scarce thing is their one irreversible action.
